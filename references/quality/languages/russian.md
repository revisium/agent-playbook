# Russian Language Profile

This conditional profile supplements `../natural-language-authoring.md` with
problems specific to professional Russian. Load it only under the selection
rules in that core reference.

## Russian-Specific Rules

- [DECISION] Do not carry English word order or a literal calque into Russian.
  If a phrase sounds unnatural, reconstruct the whole sentence around its
  meaning instead of trying to preserve the syntax of the English source.
- [DECISION] Passive and impersonal constructions are acceptable when the actor
  is genuinely unknown or irrelevant. Rewrite them when they hide who acts or
  make a simple action abstract.
- [DECISION] Replace chains of deverbal nouns with finite verbs when the meaning
  stays the same. Remove bureaucratic turns and unnecessary introductory
  phrases instead of decorating them with more qualifiers.
- [DECISION] Do not insert an English word into Russian grammar merely because
  it appeared in the source. Use a natural Russian equivalent when one exists
  and preserves the meaning.
- [DECISION] Do not impose a blanket ban on English terms. Preserve names of
  technologies, protocols, formats, APIs, types, and established professional
  terms when a translation would sound artificial or change the meaning.
- [DECISION] Avoid compound labels modeled on English, such as
  «документ-владелец», when a natural clause is clearer, such as «документ, в
  котором определено правило».
- [DECISION] Choose one Russian rendering for each concept and use it in all
  related documents. Do not translate the same source term differently in
  neighboring documents unless an explicit mapping distinguishes the concepts.

## Neutral Examples

| Плохо | Хорошо |
| --- | --- |
| «производится выполнение проверки» | «система проверяет данные» |
| «осуществить создание записи» | «создать запись» |
| «после прохождения решения человеком» | «после того как человек примет решение» |

The improved versions are not word-for-word substitutions. They restore a
natural Russian verb, word order, and actor while preserving the intended
meaning.
